Shipping & Delivery Policy

We deliver nationwide across Pakistan using TCS, Leopards Courier, and Trax Logistics.

1. Delivery Timelines

  • Karachi: 1 to 2 business days.
  • Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad: 2 to 3 business days.
  • Rest of Pakistan: 3 to 4 business days.

2. Shipping Rates

  • FREE DELIVERY: On all orders above Rs. 1,999.
  • Flat Rate Rs. 150: For single watch orders under Rs. 1,999.

3. Open Parcel Inspection

Every parcel is packed with an open parcel clearance note, authorizing the recipient to visually verify the contents before paying cash.
