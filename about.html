About ZK.PK

Pakistan's Dedicated Watch Boutique

Founded in Pakistan, ZK.PK was born out of a singular obsession: to provide authentic, high-quality, and visually breathtaking wristwatches without the exorbitant markups often seen in traditional shopping malls.

Unlike general marketplace websites that sell mobile accessories, clothes, and shoes, ZK.PK specializes EXCLUSIVELY in watches. Every model in our 40-piece collection is chosen after rigorous inspection of mechanical movement accuracy, stainless steel grade, dial craftsmanship, and water resistance seals.

We believe that trust is earned through transparency. That is why we provide Open Parcel Visual Inspection before cash payment with our nationwide courier partners (TCS, Leopards, and Trax).

Our Core Commitments:

  • 100% Genuine timepieces tested for accuracy
  • Cash on Delivery across all cities of Pakistan
  • Open parcel check permitted with rider
  • 7-day replacement warranty for mechanical defects
